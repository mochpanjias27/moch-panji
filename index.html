Profile

Teaching and researching financial mathematics at the Department of Mathematics, Universitas Padjadjaran.

I have been a lecturer in the Mathematics Study Program at Universitas Padjadjaran since June 2024, holding the academic rank of Assistant Professor (Asisten Ahli) with a research focus on financial mathematics. Before that, I worked as an IT Developer & Quality Assurance engineer for mobile banking services at Bank CIMB Niaga, an experience that shaped my interest in digital banking transaction risk.

My dissertation develops an optimization model for allocating banking funds using a Multi-Objective Robust Conditional Value-at-Risk approach. I am currently involved in research on weather derivatives, crypto investment, green portfolios, and the circular economy in the Citarum River Basin.

Research focus

Six areas, one common thread: measuring and managing uncertainty.

01

Banking Risk

Measuring and mitigating bank risk with Value-at-Risk and (Extreme) Conditional Value-at-Risk, including digital transaction and credit risk.

02

Robust Optimization

Optimization models that withstand parameter uncertainty: multi-objective robust models and polyhedral uncertainty set for fund allocation.

03

Risk Uncertainty

Modeling uncertainty through extreme value theory, fuzzy sets, and soft set for decision-making.

04

Investment Portfolios

Capital allocation across stock portfolios, green portfolios, and crypto assets under dynamic risk.

05

Operations Research

Optimization and decision modeling, from resource allocation to multi-criteria decision-making.

06

Actuarial Science

Insurance premium pricing, catastrophe bond, weather derivatives, disaster insurance, and the mathematics of pension funds.

2026·Journal of Risk and Financial ManagementVol. 19, No. 7, 465Scopus Q1First author

Deep Learning for Credit Risk Prediction in Fintech Lending: A Systematic Literature Review on Model Architectures, Imbalanced Data Handling, and Research Agenda

  • A PRISMA 2020 systematic review of 30 publications from Scopus, IEEE Xplore, and Web of Science (2015–2026).
  • Research on deep learning for fintech credit risk is growing by about 29.2% per year (CAGR), averaging 27.17 citations per document.
  • Maps four thematic clusters: P2P lending and default prediction, explainability (XAI), imbalanced data and hybrid models, and credit risk assessment.
  • The field is shifting from single architectures (2018–2020) to hybrid ensembles and XAI-based approaches (2022–2026).

2026·Journal of Risk and Financial ManagementVol. 19, No. 1, 4Scopus Q1First author

Robust Multi-Objective Optimization Model for Reserve and Credit Fund Allocation in Banking Under Conditional Value-at-Risk Constraints

  • A multi-objective robust optimization model with CVaR constraints, a polyhedral uncertainty set, and the lexicographic method.
  • Allocates bank funds to reserve funds and credit funds simultaneously while accounting for worst-case scenarios.
  • The largest reserve goes to system risk (IDR 385.8 billion); credit funds of IDR 8.38 trillion are split into micro credit 30.2%, retail 31.3%, and corporate 38.5%.
  • Offers a reference for banks, OJK, and Bank Indonesia in designing fund allocations that are both compliant and optimal.

2025·Engineering LettersVol. 33, No. 6, 1815–1823Scopus Q2First author

Optimization Framework for Allocation of Banking Funds Based on Business Risks: A Systematic Literature Review

  • Reviews bank fund allocation strategies based on credit, market, operational, and liquidity risk assessment.
  • Identifies six key publications (2010–2023) with their methods and research trends.
  • Finds a research gap: no model yet combines multi-objective robust CVaR with financial risk uncertainty.
  • Proposes a framework with three objectives: minimize reserve funds, maximize investment funds, and minimize operational funds.
Read article ↗
2023·MathematicsVol. 11, No. 16, 3507Scopus Q1First author

Reserve Fund Optimization Model for Digital Banking Transaction Risk with Extreme Value-at-Risk Constraints

  • A linear programming model that minimizes reserve funds for digital banking transactions subject to Extreme Value-at-Risk (EVaR) constraints.
  • Loss data from downtime, timeouts, external failures, and user errors prove heavy-tailed and follow the Generalized Pareto Distribution.
  • The optimal solution complies with Basel II, Basel III, and Article 71(1) of the Indonesian Limited Liability Company Law: reserves of at least 20% of capital.
  • Optimal reserve funds: IDR 643.2 billion for system risk and IDR 118.9 billion for operational risk.

2022·RisksVol. 10, No. 1, 10Scopus Q1First author

Estimation of Maximum Potential Losses for Digital Banking Transaction Risks Using the Extreme Value-at-Risks Method

  • Estimates the maximum operational-risk loss in digital banking transactions caused by system downtime and timeouts.
  • Data are simulated with MEBoot, tested for fit to the GPD with Kolmogorov–Smirnov, and EVaR is then computed with a portfolio approach.
  • The maximum potential loss reaches IDR 144.36 billion, a benchmark for the adequacy of bank reserve funds.
  • The most-cited first-author article (20 Scopus citations).
